Quantum Teleportation Protocol

Simulate the Bennett et al. (1993) three-qubit teleportation circuit. Everything runs in your browser; no backend, sign-in, or data upload is required.

01 · PrepareInput |ψ⟩ and shared Bell pair |Φ+⟩
02 · MeasureAlice applies CNOT, H, then measures two qubits
03 · CommunicateAlice sends two classical bits to Bob
04 · CorrectBob applies the appropriate Pauli correction

Input qubit

|ψ⟩ = cos(θ/2)|0⟩ + exp(iφ) sin(θ/2)|1⟩. Angles define a normalized pure state.

In an ideal noiseless simulation, correction restores Bob's state with unit fidelity (up to a global phase). Trial-to-trial variation comes from the two classical measurement bits, not from errors in the ideal protocol. This is a software simulation, not a quantum-hardware experiment.
